About the company

A newswire that keeps its sources

Worldline is the newswire and media-relations company of CommandAGI. It writes and distributes releases, keeps the journalist repertoire, pitches and monitors coverage.

Worldline exists because news needs three things that are slow to get right: the facts, the right reader and a record of who was told what. The company keeps all three in plain files on the platform, so a customer can read the whole history of its news in one place.

The company serves the other companies on CommandAGI first. Formations need a first release and a starting list of reporters. Product launches need a release, a pitch plan and a monitoring plan. Market news needs a short, checked release. Worldline also takes outside customers through the platform marketplace.

The company works through agents on CommandAGI. An executive agent runs on a schedule to find new media contacts from public sources. It opens a thread for each contact and builds the relationship file that the desk reviews. Other agents draft releases, check facts, plan pitches and read coverage. People set the line, review the output and speak for the company.

Worldline is one company in an ecosystem. Other companies on the platform sell the services that Worldline's customers need, such as accounts, design, research and logistics. Worldline does not build those services itself. It uses them through contracts that are signed before work starts.

Rules the desk works by

  1. Sources first

    Every fact, contact and mention names where it came from. A line with no source is not used.

  2. No invented claims

    A release says only what the customer can stand behind. The desk does not add a quote, a number or a reason for a reporter to care.

  3. No bought lists

    New contacts come from public sources only. The desk does not buy lists and does not scrape private groups.

  4. Stop means stop

    A journalist who asks not to be contacted is closed in the repertoire, with the request on record. No later plan contacts that person.

  5. Say what is not done

    The newswire, the repertoire and the monitoring each say what works and what is still waiting. A waiting step is never shown as finished.

Where Worldline sits

Status

Worldline is a company on CommandAGI. The newswire product is built on the platform and is tested. Sending to journalists is not switched on yet, and this site will say so until it is. The company does not claim that a release has gone out, that a reporter has agreed to cover a customer or that a story has run.

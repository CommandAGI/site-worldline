Lead story

A release is only news once the right desk reads it

By the Worldline desk · Newswire and repertoire

Worldline writes, checks and routes press releases for the companies that hire it, and keeps the list of journalists who cover each beat.

Fig. 1. Routes from one release to the outlets, aggregators and beats that cover it

Worldline is the newswire and media-relations company of CommandAGI. Companies on the platform, such as the formations we set up, product launches and market-news teams, hire Worldline to get their news in front of journalists who cover their field. The work is done by a set of agents, a small human desk and a repertoire that is kept in plain files, so every contact can be traced to where it came from.

The newswire product is built and tested on the platform. Sending to journalists is not switched on yet. Until it is, Worldline drafts, reviews and queues releases, and it tells the customer exactly which step is done. No release has gone to a journalist from this site, and no coverage is claimed here.

The executive agent of Worldline runs on a schedule. It looks through public sources for new media contacts: bylines, beat pages, newsletters, public speaker lists and outlet masthead pages. Each new name becomes a thread. In that thread an agent checks the person's beat, the outlet and the way the person prefers to be approached, then writes the relationship file that the desk reviews.

A relationship is built over time. A pitch is one step, not the goal. Worldline records each reply, each refusal and each request not to be contacted again, and it respects those records in every later plan. A journalist who says no stays in the repertoire as a closed contact, with the reason recorded.

Media monitoring follows the same rule. A coverage report lists each mention that the monitor found, the public page where it appears and the date it was checked. A mention is never counted before a person can open the page and read it.

Worldline serves the other CommandAGI companies first. It also takes outside customers through the platform marketplace. Work is priced on request and agreed in a contract before any work starts. Escrow holds the payment until the customer accepts the delivery.

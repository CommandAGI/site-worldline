Start with a brief

The fastest way to start is a short brief: the news, the facts you can stand behind, the beats you want to reach and the date you need the work done by.

Two ways in

Route one

The CommandAGI marketplace

Find Worldline through the marketplace, open a thread with the desk and send the brief there. The thread keeps the brief, the plan, the contract and the delivery in one place.

See what to ask for

Route two

Email the desk

Write to hello@worldline.commandagi.com. The desk replies with a plan, the steps it will take, what it will check and what it will not claim.

Do not send a list of private contacts or personal data about a journalist. Name the beat instead.

Brief form

Write the brief as plain text in your email or your marketplace thread. Include these lines:

  • The news, in two sentences
  • The facts, one per line, with the source of each
  • The beats and regions you want to reach, and the languages
  • Any quote, and the name of the person who agreed to it
  • The date you need the work done by
  • Anything the desk must not say

A form that sends a brief to a server is not part of this site yet. Use the marketplace or the email address above.

What happens next

  1. The desk reads the brief

    A person on the desk reads the brief and replies with a plan, or with the questions it needs answered first.

  2. A contract is signed

    The price is priced on request and is written into a contract. Both parties sign before work starts.

  3. The work is done in the thread

    You see each step as it happens, and each output is reviewed by the desk before you receive it.
